How it's built

  1. Win %. A win over an FCS team counts as 0.4 of a win here. A loss to an FCS team counts in full. The W-L column shows the real record; * marks teams with an FCS win.
  2. Opponents' win % (OWP). Each opponent's record, with its game against you removed so your result doesn't inflate or deflate it.
  3. Opponents' opponents' win % (OOWP). The average OWP of everyone you played. This catches a 6-1 opponent whose wins came against nobody.
  4. Opponent conference strength. Each conference is rated by its non-conference record vs FBS teams (shrunk toward .500 early). You get the average rating of your opponents' conferences.
  5. Against the spread. For each game: actual margin plus the team's ESPN line (DraftKings). Beating a 7-point spread by 10 is +3. Capped at ±21 per game so one fluke doesn't dominate.
  6. Margin. Point differential per game, capped at 24 so running up the score earns nothing extra. FCS games are discounted here and in the spread factor.
  7. Quality results. Bonus for wins over teams rated top 10, 11–25 and 26–50; penalty for losses to teams rated 51+ and to FCS teams.

Default weights: win % 35, opponents' win % 25, opponents' opponents' win % 10, conference strength 10, against the spread 10, margin 5, quality results 5. FCS opponents are treated as a .300 team in a weak conference. SOS rank combines OWP and OOWP at 2:1.

Tune the weights

Drag to change how much each factor counts. Rankings, movement and the bubble update instantly. Weights are rescaled to total 100%.

Data: final scores and ESPN closing spreads (DraftKings) for all 575 completed games involving FBS teams. Records match official standings for all 138 teams.
