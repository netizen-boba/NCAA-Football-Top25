How the current Top 25 is built

  1. Win % (30%). A win over an FCS team counts as 0.4 of a win here. A loss to an FCS team counts in full. The W-L column shows the real record; * marks teams with an FCS win.
  2. Opponents' win % (25%). Each opponent's record, with its game against you removed so your result doesn't inflate or deflate it.
  3. Opponents' opponents' win % (5%). The average of your opponents' win %. This catches a 6-1 opponent whose wins came against nobody.
  4. Opponent conference strength (10%). Each conference is rated by its non-conference record vs FBS teams (shrunk toward .500 early). You get the average rating of your opponents' conferences.
  5. Quality results (20%). Bonus for wins over teams rated top 10, 11–25 and 26–50; penalty for losses to teams rated 51+ and to FCS teams.
  6. Upset losses (5%). Extra cost for losing a game you were favored to win, scaled by the ESPN pregame line: nothing for losing as an underdog or in a pick'em, about 0.4 for losing as a 7.5-point favorite, 0.6 as a 13.5-point favorite, 0.8 as a 19.5-point favorite. Penalties add up; 1.5 points' worth of upset losses wipes out the factor.
  7. Short wins (5%). A cost for winning as the favorite by less than the ESPN spread, with the spread capped at 14 points. Each such win costs (capped spread − winning margin) ÷ 14: nothing for winning by 14 or more, or by the full spread; 0.14 for a 10-point win as a 12-point favorite; 0.79 for a 1-point win as a 12-point favorite. Costs add up; 4 points' worth wipes out the factor. Losses are covered by upset losses.
  8. Margin (0% by default). Off by default since short wins replaced it; use its slider to add it back. Point differential per game, capped at 24 so running up the score earns nothing extra. FCS games are discounted.

This poll rewards results only. Stats that mainly predict future games, like yards per play and results against the point spread, feed the projection instead. With these weights the higher-ranked team won 82% of the FBS games already played. FCS opponents are treated as a .300 team in a weak conference. SOS rank combines opponents' and opponents' opponents' win % at 2:1.

How the projection works

  1. Team ratings in points. Half comes from performance: opponent-adjusted scoring margin (capped at 28 a game) and opponent-adjusted yards per play, converted to points, weighted 1:3 because yards per play predicted better. The other half comes from the betting market: the team strengths that best explain the ESPN point spreads of games already played. Home field is worth about 3 points.
  2. Win chance for each remaining game. From the rating gap, assuming real margins land within about 15 points of the prediction two times in three. When ESPN has posted a line for the game, the line is used instead.
  3. Simulate the rest of the season. Every remaining regular-season game is played out thousands of times, with each team's rating also allowed to be a bit off in each run. This gives the Top 25 odds, unbeaten odds and the 80% range of final ranks.
  4. Rank the projected season. The projected W-L is one typical season. It starts with every favorite winning, then hands games to the underdog, choosing the likeliest upsets, until each team's record is within about one game of its expected wins (the sum of its win chances). About a third of remaining games are expected to go to the underdog, so the projection includes upsets rather than having favorites win out. That season is ranked by the current-Top-25 formula at its default weights, so a team's projected rank always matches its projected record. The sim range shows how far a team could rise or fall.

Tested on 2026 games, fitting only on earlier weeks (weeks 4–9, 308 games): the ratings picked 66% of winners, and the ESPN line picked 72%. That gap narrowed from early October onward as the ratings got more games, which is why posted lines are used for next week's games.

Data: final scores, box-score yards and plays, and ESPN pregame spreads for all 624 completed games involving FBS teams. Records are checked against official standings for all 138 teams on every update.
